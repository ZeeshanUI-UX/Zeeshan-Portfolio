The Challenge

Bringing care into one clear flow.

Clinics were juggling appointments, treatments and payments across disconnected tools, and patients had no single place to book, follow and pay for their care.

Staff needed to see the day at a glance; patients needed booking and payment to feel effortless on a phone.

The goal: one product family — a patient app and a staff dashboard — built on the same flows and the same components.

The Process

Mapping every patient moment.

I mapped the patient, appointment, treatment and payment journeys end to end with the clinic team, marking where people waited, repeated information or dropped off.

The Structure

Two products, one system.

The patient mobile app and the staff admin dashboard share one component library, so a booking looks and behaves the same wherever it appears.

The Solution

Simple steps, clear status.

Booking and payment became short step-by-step flows with clear progress. The dashboard opens on today’s schedule and surfaces what needs action first.

The Outcome

Three things shaped
the design.

  1. 01

    One place for care

    Appointments, treatments and payments live in a single patient journey instead of separate tools.

  2. 02

    Built for clinic staff

    The admin dashboard puts today’s schedule and pending actions first, so staff spend less time searching.

  3. 03

    One design system

    Shared components keep the web dashboard and mobile app consistent and quicker to build.
