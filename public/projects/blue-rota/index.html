The Challenge

Making police pay rules readable.

Police officers work rotating shifts under detailed Federation rules for overtime, rest days, bank holidays and part-time pay. Most never know what they are owed, and their families can’t plan around the rota.

The rules themselves change: new pay scales each September, new bank holidays and high-demand days every year.

The goal: admins set the rules once, officers see their real numbers, and partners see the schedule.

The Process

The rules become the spec.

I turned the Federation rules into admin screens: pay scales by rank and version, bank holidays and high-demand days with multipliers, leave by years of service, six overtime rule types and part-time pro-rata formulas.

The Structure

Three products, one engine.

The admin dashboard holds the rules, a calculation layer turns them into hourly rates and multipliers, the officer app shows the results, and Partner Sync links an officer to a partner.

The Solution

Your shift, your numbers.

The Today tab shows a live shift ring with Extend Shift, plus leave, payday and overtime at a glance. Overtime entries move from Pending to Put Through, with the right rate applied.

The Outcome

Three things shaped
the design.

  1. 01

    Rules in one place

    Admins update pay scales and multipliers once; the apps use them from the effective date.

  2. 02

    Overtime made visible

    Officers see what each entry earns and what is still pending, and learn the rights they lose money on.

  3. 03

    Family in the loop

    Partner Sync shows both rotas side by side, with the days you are both free.
