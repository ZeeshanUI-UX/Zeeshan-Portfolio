The Challenge

Daily care that never feels like a chore.

Pet apps often nag: streaks at risk, sad pets, pushy upgrades. LucoPets wanted the opposite, a calm place to plan care and keep a pet’s whole story.

It had to cover daily tasks, health records, rewards, memories, family sharing and, with real care, grief when a pet passes.

The goal: warm, soft and reassuring screens where nothing earned is ever taken away.

The Process

Rules from 111 client answers.

I turned the client’s answers into design principles every screen follows: calm, never negative; nothing earned is taken away; the garden is never locked; upgrade asks are gentle; grief is handled with care.

The Structure

A soft sidebar, eleven places.

A floating 120 px sidebar holds Dashboard, Planner, Pet Profile, Garden, Timeline, Celebrations, Capsules, Care Circle, Memorial, Rewards and Settings. A pet switcher in the top bar changes the active pet everywhere.

The Solution

Care grows a garden.

Days of care grow the Care Garden through journeys with milestones at 25, 50, 75, 90 and 100%. Finished journeys become a Journey Book, then porcelain keepsakes in a cabinet.

The Outcome

Three things shaped
the design.

  1. 01

    Calm by design

    No “streak at risk”, no sad pets. Mood words are only Attentive and Content.

  2. 02

    Free stays generous

    Premium changes what can be created next; memories, badges and the garden stay free.

  3. 03

    A ceramic look

    Soft 3D porcelain, warm cream and teal, with neumorphic buttons and real pet photos.
