The Challenge

Making data-heavy work feel manageable.

DEUT covers inventory, budgeting, procurement and asset management — four modules full of dense tables and multi-step approvals.

Teams needed to find a record, understand its status and act on it without wading through every column.

The goal: a consistent table and approval pattern that works across all four modules.

The Process

Untangling the approval chains.

I mapped each module’s approval flow — who requests, who reviews, who signs off — and found the steps the modules had in common.

The Structure

One pattern, four modules.

Every module uses the same table layout, filters and record panel, so learning one module means knowing them all.

The Solution

Tables you can actually scan.

Smart defaults, saved filters and clear status chips make large tables readable, and approvals move through a visible step-by-step tracker.

The Outcome

Three things shaped
the design.

  1. 01

    Consistent modules

    Inventory, budgeting, procurement and assets share one layout and component set.

  2. 02

    Readable data

    Status chips, column priorities and saved filters cut long tables down to what matters.

  3. 03

    Visible approvals

    Multi-step approvals show who has signed off and what is waiting, at every stage.
