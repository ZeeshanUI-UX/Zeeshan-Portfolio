The Challenge

Construction runs on paper.

A subcontractor’s back office lives in inboxes, spreadsheets and folders of signed PDFs. AP teams retyped vendor bills, chased lien waivers by hand and found out at month end that balances didn’t match.

Nobody had one view of contract, billed, collected and cost per project, and the same invoice arriving from two inboxes could be paid twice.

The goal: one place where every bill, waiver and dollar is tied to the job it came from, and paying is one click.

The Process

Two connections, six short steps.

First-time setup asks for only two things: the inbox where bills arrive and the accounting platform. A new project is a six-step wizard, or one upload: drop a signed contract and Sledge fills the project in.

The Structure

Every job on one map.

Projects sit in the middle of everything. They open as cards beside a live map, and each one has a photo banner with contract amount, client and timeline, plus Home and Accounting tabs.

The Solution

From inbox to paid in four moves.

Bills move through Importing, Needs review, Approved and Paid. Review puts the PDF beside the form so nothing is retyped, duplicates are flagged before approval, and paying a batch goes through Stripe with the fee shown first.

The Outcome

the design.
